Two-stage training on tiled SAR data

Stage 1: VisDrone pretraining

50 epochs at 640×640, giving 0.396 mAP@50 on its own validation set.

Stage 2: HERIDAL + SARD tiles

94 epochs, cosine schedule. Mosaic, copy-paste at 0.3, colour jitter and rotation up to 15°.

Backgrounds were kept as negative tiles, about 15% of the total.

Training is two-stage. First, VisDrone, a general aerial benchmark. Then fine-tuning on HERIDAL and SARD, cut into 640 by 640 tiles. Tiling keeps a 64-pixel person at its native size instead of shrinking it. The validation split has 2,424 tiles and 3,445 people, and I will report results on it overall and by source.
